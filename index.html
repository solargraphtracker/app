What3words

what3words gives every 3 metre square on Earth its own three-word address, like ///filled.count.soap. It’s much easier to note down and share than coordinates, and it’s free to use.

In this app, it’s how you record exactly where a camera is hidden. Find your spot in the what3words app, copy its three words, and paste them into the what3words address field when you add or edit a camera. You can paste a what3words link too.
