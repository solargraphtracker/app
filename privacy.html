Solargraph tracker: Privacy

Solargraph tracker is a free, personal app for keeping track of pinhole cameras. It has no server and no accounts of its own, and it does not collect, sell or share any data.

Your data stays with you. Your cameras, notes and photos are stored in your own browser on your own device.

Google Drive backup (optional). If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app saves one backup file to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, using only the permission drive.appdata. The app can only see that one folder, never your other files. The backup goes directly between your device and your Drive; nobody else, including the developer, can access it. You can disconnect at any time in the Account tab.

The app uses no analytics or advertising.
