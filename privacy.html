Solargraph tracker: Privacy

Solargraph tracker is a free, personal app for keeping track of pinhole cameras. It has no server and no accounts of its own, and it does not collect, sell or share any data.

Your data stays with you. Your cameras, notes and photos are stored in your own browser on your own device.

Cloud sync (optional). If you sign in with Google Drive or Dropbox, the app keeps a copy of your cameras and photos in a private app folder in your own account, so your devices stay in step. With Google it uses only the drive.appdata permission, so it can see that one hidden folder and none of your other files; with Dropbox it uses its own app folder (Apps > Solargraph tracker). Data goes directly between your device and your cloud account; nobody else, including the developer, can access it. You can sign out at any time in the Backup tab.

The app uses no analytics or advertising.
